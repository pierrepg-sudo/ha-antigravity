Delete a saved conversation
Type /resume in the CLI and press Enter. Use ↑/↓ to highlight a conversation, open More keys, then tap Delete conversation (F4). Check the CLI confirmation before pressing Enter; Esc cancels.
F4 acts on the active terminal. Use it only in the conversation picker. It does not clear the history snapshot.
